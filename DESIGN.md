# Editorial portfolio design

The user selected a light magazine aesthetic: black and white, generous space, refined typography, and a product portfolio focus.

- Paper `#fcfcfa`, ink `#20201e`, secondary text `#686863`, rules `#d9d9d2`, secondary surface `#f2f2ed`.
- Self-hosted Instrument Serif for English display text, a Noto Serif SC subset for Chinese display text, and the system sans-serif stack for body copy.
- One large typographic opening, an asymmetric featured image and story, two related project entries, then a quieter about section and concise experience list.
- Preserve factual experiences. Linux is an exploration direction. The supplied concept image is labelled as AI-assisted concept art and does not claim to show the delivered prototype.
- Local assets, keyboard navigation, semantic headings, native project disclosure controls, and reduced-motion support.

## Skills researched

- [Anthropic frontend-design](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md): adopted its deliberate typography, single expressive focal point, subject-specific imagery, and reduction of repetitive decorative labels.
- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable): researched as a design-review option; its executable workflow is not installed in this project.
- [UI UX Pro Max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill): researched as an alternative catalogue for design and accessibility guidance.

Font licenses are included in `assets/`.
